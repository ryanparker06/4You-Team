03 / THE STORY

THE HISTORY
OF THE 4YOU TEAM.

On Thursday, 23 March 2023 at 16:54, the first 4You Project was created. On that specific date at that specific time, Ryan and OMJO crossed paths and created the Bump4You Official.

However... that server never started as Bump4You Official. The server started as an advertising server, with Ryan handling the Server/Staff side and OMJO handling the Bot side.

Overtime, what is now forgotten, the advertise server vanished and Bump4You was established. And, over the last few years Ryan and OMJO worked non-stop on making Bump4You the most efficient, most unique, and entirely free service.

Coming around to the last few months, we've started to take on more projects, like Ban(ana) and Ticket4You. With an aim to provide free high-end Discord Bots to communities.

We can't wait to see what the future brings, and what you bring!
